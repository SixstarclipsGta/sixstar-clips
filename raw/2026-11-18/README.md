# Raw clips, launch night 18 to 19 Nov 2026

The original PS5 uploads from the launch-night chat, in the order Zubair sent them (22:53 on 18 Nov to 05:38 on 19 Nov, Berlin time). 1920x1080, 59.94 fps, about 30 s each unless noted. No exact duplicates were found among the 30 files (md5sum).

The folder is dated 2026-11-18 because the session started that evening; `raw/2026-11-19/` holds the later sessions of 19 Nov.

Times are seconds into the raw file. "Used" is the stretch that went into the short. Short names are files on branch `clips` (`clips/2026-11-19-<name>.mp4`).
